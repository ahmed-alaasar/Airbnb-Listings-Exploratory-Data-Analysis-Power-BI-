# Descriptive Analysis

The analysis focuses on listing prices and the city/neighborhood comparisons shown in the Power BI report.

## Price Analysis

The requested descriptive measures are:

- Mean price
- Median price
- Maximum price
- Minimum price
- Count of listings with a $0 price
- Count of listings with a blank price

The before/after report displays these price KPIs and separates blank-priced listings from zero-priced listings.

## City Price Analysis

A city-level summary is used to calculate:

- Mean listing price by city
- Median listing price by city

The city-level median is the reference used for the documented blank-price imputation process. See [Price Cleaning and Imputation](../Data_Cleaning/Price_Imputation.md).

## Other Report Comparisons

The dashboard also shows listing counts and average host acceptance rate by city, plus neighborhood-level average price and review score. The screenshot comparison records the visible values and interpretation notes in [Report Findings](../ReportShots/EDA_Comparison.md).
